STAR stories (**S**ituation, **T**ask, **A**ction, **R**esult), each tagged with the themes it answers. Aim for **6 to 8 strong stories** that together cover every theme below; one story can serve 2 or 3 themes.

- Keep each story to **2 to 3 minutes** spoken. The review target is 3 minutes, told out loud without notes.
- Lead with *your* actions ("I"), and end with a measurable result and what you learned.
- New story: `prep new star <slug> "Title"`, then add `themes: [conflict, teamwork]` in its frontmatter.
