## How the mix is chosen

1. **Reviews first.** Every review due in the next 7 days gets a slot, placed on its due date.
2. **New vs review share** starts at 70/30. It moves toward review when the review backlog is large, after 3+ regressions in 14 days, 4 weeks before a booked interview (50/50) and 2 weeks before it (30/70).
3. **Spare review slots** go to problems in your weakest patterns, even when they are not due yet.
4. **New problems** come from NeetCode 150. Each slot goes to the unlocked pattern with the highest weakness score (at most 4 per pattern per week), Easy before Medium before Hard. Advanced Graphs, 2-D DP and Tries unlock after 3 problems in Graphs, 1-D DP and Trees respectively.
5. **Interleaving:** new problems are dealt out round-robin by pattern, so the same pattern never runs back to back.
